# HouseJet edition — real estate rules

This is the HouseJet edition of Vibe Editing, set up for **real estate agents**. Everything in
this file is a standing rule. It applies to every clip, every highlight and every caption, on
top of the editing playbooks in `plugins/vibe-editing/skills/edit/`.

The person using this kit is a licensed agent making content for **their own** brand and
brokerage. HouseJet built the kit; the clips belong to the agent.

## 1. Setup interview (real estate)

During onboarding step 4 ("apply their brand"), ask these one at a time and save the answers to
`brand/agent-profile.json`:

1. Your name as it should appear on clips.
2. Your brokerage's exact legal name (it goes on every clip).
3. Your logo and your brokerage logo (drag them into `brand/logos/`).
4. Your brand colors and the caption look you want (a screenshot into `brand/caption-style/`).
5. Your market: city and the neighborhoods you work.
6. Who you mostly help: buyers, sellers, first-time buyers, luxury, investors, relocation.
7. How a clip should OPEN: a 2-second hook that names who it is for
   (example: "If you're buying in [city] this year…").
8. How a clip should END: your call to action
   (example: "Comment HOME and I'll send you the list").
9. Music: royalty-free only. Point to their tracks in `brand/music/` or use none.

**Skip Frame.io + Monday delivery** unless the agent asks for it. Agents post to their own
social accounts; they do not need a review board.

## 2. Fair Housing (never break these)

- Never select, cut or caption a clip so it says or implies who a home or area is "good for"
  based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, familial status, or any
  other class protected by federal, state or local law.
- Flag (do not ship) any line that uses steering language, including: "safe neighborhood",
  "perfect for families", "great for young couples", "exclusive community", "walking distance
  to [church/temple/mosque]", "no kids", "ideal for singles", or descriptions of who lives there.
- Describe the **home and its features**, never the people. Amenities are fine
  ("two blocks from the park"), demographics are not.
- When a clip is flagged, tell the agent which line, why, and offer a clean alternate cut.

## 3. Brokerage and advertising

- The brokerage name from the agent profile appears on every finished clip (end card or a
  lower-third). Never ship a clip without it.
- Most states have specific advertising rules for licensees. Tell the agent once, at setup, to
  confirm their state's rule with their managing broker. Do not give legal advice.

## 4. Numbers and claims

- Never add a price, interest rate, days-on-market, appreciation figure or any other market
  statistic to a caption, title or overlay that the agent did not say on camera.
- If the agent says a number on camera, keep it exactly as said. Do not round or "improve" it.
- Never write "guaranteed" or promise a sale price, timeline or return.

## 5. Media rights

- Only use footage, photos and music the agent shot or has rights to. MLS photos usually belong
  to the photographer or listing agent; ask before using any listing media the agent did not
  shoot.
- Royalty-free or licensed music only.

## 6. What makes a good real estate clip

When ranking moments (`plugins/vibe-editing/skills/edit/prompts/clip_select.md`), prefer:

- A direct answer to one real buyer or seller question, opened on the answer itself.
- A specific, local detail (a street, a neighborhood, a school district name, a process step)
  over generic advice.
- A listing walkthrough moment with one clear feature and a reason it matters.
- A clean ending on the agent's call to action from the profile.

Avoid: clips that open on "So…", "Hey guys", or the agent introducing themselves; clips that end
mid-explanation; anything the agent would not say to a client's face.
